running should feel fun, not lonely

Most running apps today focus on pace charts, splits, and stats. While those matter, they've made running feel like a solo, data-heavy chore.

We're reimagining running as something social, rewarding, and exciting. With hyperlocal leaderboards, small daily challenges, and a simple way to find people to run with, we're building an experience that helps you stay consistent and actually enjoy it.

Earn redeemable points, join challenges, and see where you stand in your own neighborhood. It's running, but with a community behind you.
